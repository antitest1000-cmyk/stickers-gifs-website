About StickerPencil ??

Hand-drawn charm, viral humor, and effortless cross-platform sticker sharing.

?? The Sketchbook Vision

In a digital landscape filled with generic AI-generated graphics and glossy corporate icons, StickerPencil was born out of a desire for something tangible, playful, and charming: the timeless feel of an artist�s desk, complete with pencil scribbles, washi tape strips, highlighter accents, and hand-drawn doodles.

Every sticker on our platform is curated to bring joy, humor, and expressive personality to everyday chat interactions across WhatsApp, Discord, Instagram Stories, Snapchat, Reddit, and Telegram.

We believe digital stickers should be frictionless to share. Our licensing policy is straightforward:

  • ? Free for Personal Use: You are free to copy, download, and send these stickers in any personal chat, direct message, or community group.
  • ? Discord & Community Servers: Server owners may upload these stickers as custom server emotes and stickers for their communities.

?? Copyright & DMCA Notice

StickerPencil respects the intellectual property rights of artists and content creators worldwide. We maintain a strict and expedited notice-and-takedown procedure in compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

If you believe your copyrighted artwork has been copied or uploaded without authorization, please submit a written DMCA takedown notice containing:

DMCA Takedown Notice Format:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
2. The exact URL(s) on StickerPencil where the material is located.
3. Your contact information (Full name, address, telephone number, and email address).
4. A statement affirming your good-faith belief that the disputed use is unauthorized.
5. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the notification is accurate and you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

?? Send DMCA inquiries directly to: dmca@stickerpencil.com
