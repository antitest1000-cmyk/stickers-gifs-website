Why GIFs Struggle with Transparency (The Technical Reason)

The GIF format was created in 1987 and only supports 1-bit binary transparency. A pixel is either 100% opaque or 100% transparent � there is no partial transparency (anti-aliasing). When an artist draws a smooth curved line, GIF encoders surround the edge with a harsh white pixel fringe.

By contrast, StickerPencil utilizes modern PNG and WebP with 8-bit alpha transparency (256 levels of opacity). When you paste a sticker into Discord, WhatsApp, or Instagram Stories, the edges blend smoothly against any background color or photo.
