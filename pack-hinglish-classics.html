About the Hinglish Classics Bundle

Hinglish Classics is a vibrant collection of 382 transparent PNG/GIF stickers that blend the punchy charm of Hindi slang with the breezy flow of English. Each sticker captures iconic catchphrases, viral dialogues, and everyday reactions you hear on Indian group chats, rendered in bold typography and lively illustrations that pop on any screen.

Designed for WhatsApp, Discord, Instagram Stories, Snapchat, Reddit and Telegram, this pack turns ordinary conversations into comedy gold. Whether you’re dropping a ‘Kya Baat Hai!’ after a win, a sarcastic ‘Beta, chill’ during drama, or a dramatic ‘Mujhe laga tha…’ for meme moments, your friends will instantly recognize the cultural vibe and keep the chat buzzing. Perfect for meme‑hungry groups, study circles, and family threads that love a good desi punchline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add Hinglish Classics stickers to WhatsApp? ↓

Download the zip, open WhatsApp > Settings > Stickers > My Stickers > Add New. Select the folder and the stickers appear instantly.

Can I use these stickers on Discord with Nitro? ↓

Yes! Upload the PNG/GIF files as custom emojis in your server. Each emoji must be under 256KB to meet Nitro requirements.

What does ‘transparent PNG’ mean for these stickers? ↓

Transparent PNGs have an invisible background, so the sticker blends seamlessly over any chat background or story frame.

Are the stickers free for personal use and can I use them commercially? ↓

The pack is free for personal chats, social media stories, and Discord servers. For commercial projects (ads, merch), contact StickerPencil.com for a licensing fee.
