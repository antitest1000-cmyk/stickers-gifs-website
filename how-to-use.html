How to Use Custom Stickers Everywhere

Each app handles transparent stickers slightly differently. Follow these hand-drawn cheatsheets for WhatsApp, Discord, Instagram, Snapchat, Reddit & Telegram.

??

How to Use on WhatsApp

Desktop & Mobile Guide
  1. 1
    Desktop (WhatsApp Web / Desktop App): Click the "?? Copy" button on any sticker on StickerPencil. Head to your WhatsApp Web chat and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac). WhatsApp will automatically recognize the transparent PNG and let you send it directly!
  2. 2
    Mobile (iPhone & Android): Click "?? Save" to download the transparent PNG to your Photos/Gallery. In WhatsApp chat, tap the sticker icon > tap the "+" or "Create" button > select the saved PNG from your gallery. WhatsApp auto-trims and turns it into a permanent personal sticker.
  3. 3
    Bulk Import Entire Packs: Download an entire pack from our Packs page, then open the free "Sticker.ly" app and tap "Create Pack" > "Select All" to sync 30+ stickers directly into your WhatsApp keyboard.

Pro Tip: All StickerPencil images have transparent alpha channels, so they blend seamlessly without ugly white square borders!

How to Use on Discord

Server Emojis & Nitro Stickers
  1. 1
    Quick Chat Reaction: Click "?? Copy" on StickerPencil, switch over to Discord, and press Ctrl+V. Send it as an inline transparent image reaction in any channel or DM!
  2. 2
    Server Custom Emoji / Sticker: If you manage a server (or have emoji permissions), go to Server Settings > Emoji or Stickers > click "Upload Emoji". Pick your downloaded StickerPencil file, name your shortcut (e.g. :crying_cat:), and your entire community can use it!
??

How to Use on Instagram Stories & Reels

Photo Sticker Overlay Tool
  1. 1
    Download to Camera Roll: Tap "?? Save" on any sticker on your phone so it is saved to your camera roll.
  2. 2
    Add as Story Overlay: Open Instagram > swipe right to create a Story. Take a photo or select a video. Tap the Sticker Tray icon at the top right > scroll down and look for the Photo / Gallery Sticker icon (looks like two overlapping pictures with a plus sign). Select your downloaded sticker, and pinch-zoom to position it anywhere!
??

How to Use on Snapchat

Snap Scissors & Camera Roll Sticker
  1. 1
    Save Sticker: Save the transparent sticker to your device photos.
  2. 2
    Import into Snap: Take a snap in Snapchat > tap the Sticker / Paperclip icon on the right tool column > tap the Camera Roll tab > select your sticker. Snapchat will overlay it with full transparency so you can doodle or stick it onto anything!
??

Reddit & Telegram

Comments & Bot Creator
  1. 1
    Reddit Comments: In subreddits with image-enabled comments (like r/memes, r/aww, r/gaming), click the image icon in the comment box and upload the downloaded PNG to post high-res sticker reactions!
  2. 2
    Telegram Stickers Bot: Open Telegram and search for @Stickers bot. Send /newpack, give it a title, then upload your downloaded PNG stickers one by one with a matching emoji. You'll receive a custom t.me/addstickers/yourpack link to share with anyone!
