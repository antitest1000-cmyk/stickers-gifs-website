Method 1: The 1-Click Clipboard Shortcut (WhatsApp Web & Desktop)

If you use WhatsApp on Chrome, Edge, Brave, Safari, or the official WhatsApp Windows/Mac app, this is the single fastest trick:

  1. Open StickerPencil.com or our dedicated WhatsApp Stickers Hub.
